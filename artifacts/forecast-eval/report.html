How to interpret this run

Simulator. Independent future home/away goal counts. Training games supply scoring rates and half-duration distributions. The stronger variant initializes rates from the frozen pregame market. Exact Poisson probabilities remove simulation noise.

Limits. Archived FIFA timestamps are not verified receipt times. The model ignores player movements, cards, tactical changes and shot quality. Market probabilities are sampled and normalized. This is one tournament with only 20 test games.

Confidence intervals resample whole matches, not snapshots. Shared teams may still induce dependence. The test set should not become a tuning target.
