# Independent review instructions

Open `review.html` for frozen source rules and CSV export, or fill `review-blind.csv` directly. Avoid opening `predictions/`, `responses/`, or method comparisons before completing labels.

Compare Kalshi YES with the explicitly selected Polymarket outcome, not with an assumed YES token. Record your name/identifier, date, and source-clause evidence.

1. **Identity:** same event or indicator, same reference time/period, same selected participant/outcome, and same advertised threshold bucket? Label `match`, `nonmatch`, or `unknown`. Source/aggregation/exception differences belong in settlement when the advertised target is otherwise the same. A winner is different from covering a spread. Opposite team outcomes are different. Game rematches on different dates are different.
2. **Settlement:** can you give an explicit scenario with different payouts? Label `different` and state the scenario with cited clauses. If complete relevant terms establish equivalent payouts, label `compatible` and cite source/time, comparator and exception coverage. Otherwise label `unknown`; absent evidence is not a counterexample.

Potential divergences include cancellation/postponement, a tie, overtime, missing releases, fallback sources, candle versus average/index, exact versus rounded rate changes, and strict versus inclusive cutoffs. Generic PDF examples do not bind this market's parameter values. Do not use close/expiration timestamps as substitutes for the stated observation time.

Two reviewers should work from separate copies. A final adjudicated sheet should retain both reviewer identities and document disagreements. To score a sample, return only the completed rows; incomplete rows remain unscored. All 400 rows are needed for a fully adjudicated benchmark, but a blinded sample is useful initial evidence and must be reported as a sample.
