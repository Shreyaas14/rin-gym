**Why high win rate loses money.** The completed fresh original replay wins 57.7% of traded match-replays, but average win is $0.209 and average loss is $0.519. Its 120 winning replays earn $25.06, while 88 losing replays lose $45.68: net **−$20.62**. At that fixed conditional payout mix, break-even would require about **71.3%** wins. This is arithmetic on the observed mix, not a probability threshold or a promise about another policy.

The exact accounting reconstruction gives gross P&L before modeled fees of −$18.0465 and midpoint accounting P&L before modeled fees/spread of −$15.0825. Costs worsen an already negative result; these are same-fill accounting decompositions, not maker counterfactual returns. Model probabilities also score worse than contemporaneous market forecasts. Some actions disagree with those probabilities, but the consistency intervention did not solve the later-period loss. The conservative executor changed selection, exits, policy prompting and sizing; its full effect cannot be attributed to smaller bets.

**Maker/taker implementation.** The [execution core](/Users/alexkan/Desktop/frontiers/rin-gym-execution-aware/app/trade_env/execution.py) adds separate maker/taker fee schedules and supported rebates, explicit validity/provenance, fail-closed unknown historical fees, book depth and constraints, per-fill liquidity roles, post-only arrival rejection, partial/unfilled orders, latency, cancellation, signal expiry and queue assumptions. [The evaluation harness](/Users/alexkan/Desktop/frontiers/rin-gym-execution-aware/experiments/execution_aware/evaluate.py) preserves exact legacy reproduction and compares controlled frozen-signal taker/maker/hybrid policies with the same initial fee-aware intent gate.

Hybrid waits for cancellation, then checks remaining quantity, observed costs and signal validity before crossing. Known announced fee changes are included conservatively without using later fee evidence to select an earlier order. Executable bid/ask prices embed spread; accounting never debits spread again. Net equals gross minus fees plus rebates; midpoint accounting also exposes spread/slippage. Raw intents, stage quotes, fill quantities/roles, fees/rebates, limits, timestamps, cancellation and unfilled reasons are saved. Actual residual inventory is carried after partial fills.

Current official [fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees), [market details](https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/market-details), [rebates](https://docs.polymarket.com/programs/maker-rebates) and [orders](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/place-orders) were checked. Minimum-size units also differ between current Gamma and book documentation; one share, worth at most $1, fails either interpretation of 5. These are current-snapshot feasibility findings, not proof of historical constraints. Rebates remain zero because eligibility/allocation evidence is absent. Five-decimal precision is supported by current documentation; the implementation's half-up tie rule is an explicit modeling convention, not separately verified exchange rounding.

No observed historical bid/ask books, sizes, aggressor trades or queue position exist in the inspected release. The harness reads only cohort- and token-filtered historical prices, strictly as of each decision; it never supplies a future execution fill to order selection. Its invented spread/depth/opposing flow/fill fractions are explicit pessimistic/central/optimistic sensitivities. They are not verified maker fills or guaranteed P&L bounds. Fixed-60-second price changes after assumed fills are reported as historical-price proxies, not measured adverse selection of real fills.
