## Question and reason to choose it

Do pregame bookmaker prices identify Polymarket 90-minute soccer contracts whose expected payout exceeds an executable taker purchase price and fees? The existing model trails the market, calibration does not close the gap, and maker gains are unvalidated sensitivities. This experiment introduces an external information source and tests a directly executable decision rather than assuming that a model repair creates alpha. It is a disagreement strategy, not guaranteed arbitrage. Bookmakers can be wrong or share information; two sources are not statistically independent.

## Cohort and immutable rules

- Plumbing phase: first 20 future fixtures across English Premier League, Spanish La Liga, Italian Serie A and German Bundesliga after collection is enabled. Use these only to validate mappings, timestamps, quota accounting and execution constraints; do not inspect their outcomes or use them to tune thresholds. Require at least 16 complete, valid captures or stop as a data-feasibility failure.
- Assessment: next 1,000 scheduled fixtures from those four leagues, or 270 calendar days from assessment start, whichever comes first. Freeze the chronological fixture manifest in advance of each fixture, with scheduled kickoff, canonical IDs and selection timestamps. Include every candidate in the primary denominator, including missing data, no signal and unfilled orders. Postponed/void matches are logged and accounted according to the actual contract's refund rules; no replacement fixtures to improve results.
- All matches must be explicitly approved as development by the exclusion guard before collection. Uncertain or protected membership blocks the record. Do not enroll, open, score or alter the protected holdout. Record guard heads and development manifests. The existing guard receipt has state pending_enrollment and zero protected IDs; do not mistake this for an established protected evaluation cohort.
- References fixed to Pinnacle (`pinnacle`) and Betfair Sportsbook UK (`betfair_sb_uk`), not Betfair Exchange. No bookmaker substitution after observing returns. Coverage and authorized free access must be demonstrated before launch.
- Capture decimal home/draw/away odds at scheduled kickoff minus 11 and minus 10 minutes. Match home/away orientation and regulation-time settlement rules exactly. Use only data received before the decision, with provider update age at most 90 seconds. Archive source timestamps and local receipt timestamps. Require each source's probability for the selected outcome to change by at most 0.01 between captures and the two sources to differ by at most 0.03 at the decision. This limits stale-reference risk; provider timestamps do not prove original source freshness.
- For each source, remove the quoted overround by the fixed proportional rule `p_i = (1 / decimal_odds_i) / sum_j(1 / decimal_odds_j)`. This is an explicit probability approximation, not verified fair value. Use the smaller of the two probabilities for entry gating. Use their mean vector for secondary forecasting diagnostics.
- At minus 10 minutes choose at most one YES outcome per match: the largest positive conservative net edge; deterministic tie order home, draw, away. No LLM forecast, portfolio-conditioned prompt, later re-entry, early exit, parameter fitting or regime selection.

## Executable policy and accounting

- Paper taker-only purchase, hold through settlement. Request the smallest valid native order satisfying the contemporaneous minimum quantity/notional and precision constraints, subject to at most $10 total paper cash outlay including fees. If minimum-size units or fees cannot be established, skip with an explicit reason. No actual funds are committed.
- At the decision use a fresh recorded full-depth ask book, at most one second old, to estimate VWAP and applicable market-specific taker fees. Set a marketable limit no more than one actual market tick above the decision's best ask. Require the smaller reference probability minus estimated price, fee per share and the one-tick adverse-price allowance to be at least **0.05 dollars per share**. This five-cent margin is fixed before results and is not a claim that probability error is bounded by five cents.
- Fill-and-kill execution; no resting remainder. Model arrival after the larger of two seconds or the measured conservative network/processing latency, plus any market-specific matching delay. Freeze the measured latency value from plumbing before assessment. Walk observed arrival-time depth under the original limit, retaining partial and missed fills. Do not use future books to choose earlier orders. Require continuous collection sufficient to reconstruct arrivals; gaps mean no credited fill.
- A displayed book can change before a real order arrives. These are **approximate taker paper returns**, not actual executions. Also compute a deliberately conservative fill-selection scenario that retains losing apparent fills and removes profitable apparent fills. It is a warning diagnostic, not an achievable policy or a promotion gate. Live fill validation remains necessary after paper success.
- Obtain and archive fee rules effective at each fill, including market ID, rates, exponent, precision, effective interval and acquisition timestamp. Unknown schedules block fills. Rebates are zero. Current generic sports fees are not retrospectively substituted. Settlement cash flow follows the archived contract rules; no invented exit spread or sale is charged when held to payout.
- Entry ask prices already include spread; do not charge it twice. Reconcile net P&L = execution-price gross P&L - fees + verified rebates. Separately attribute midpoint-to-execution spread/slippage, and log stake, capital lockup, fees, intended/filled quantities, missing captures and unfilled reasons.
- Mandatory stress replay: retain the original signals and limits but add two seconds to arrival latency and one tick of adverse execution price on any remaining fill, within the original limit. Recompute fees and partial/missed fills. Never credit improved fills from this stress. Stress is an approximation; report its limits.

## Success, failure and stop

Primary statistic: net paper P&L per **all assessment candidates**, paired against zero-cash/no-trade, with all contracts and observations from a match kept together. Preregister 10,000 bootstrap replicates and seed 20261013. Compute a one-sided 95% lower bound both by whole-match resampling and by contiguous two-calendar-week block resampling of the chronological record; use the smaller bound. Time blocks address some shared-team/calendar dependence, not every dependence source.

Declare paper success only if:

1. At least 200 distinct matches have a nonzero fill, at least 20 calendar weeks are represented, and at least 80% of all assessment candidates have complete valid source/book/fee captures.
2. The smaller lower confidence bound is strictly positive in **both** the primary replay and the mandatory latency/one-tick stress replay.

Positive mean P&L with a bound crossing zero is inconclusive. A nonpositive mean is negative. Too few trades or insufficient data is inconclusive/data failure, not success. These sample sizes do not guarantee statistical power; the traded frequency, dependence and payoff variance are unknown before collection. No intermediate efficacy looks, early positive stopping, extensions, threshold changes, alternative bookmakers or cohort replacements. Stop at the fixed fixture/time boundary and abandon this registered variant if it fails either success condition. Immediately stop collection on a protection violation, unbounded billing exposure or loss of provenance. No paper-loss stopping rule is used, because outcome-based truncation would bias this fixed-horizon analysis.

Report net/gross P&L, fees, spread/slippage, requested/filled quantity, turnover, maximum cash exposure and capital duration, average win/loss, worst losses, profitable-match rate over all candidates and traded matches, position win rate, fixed 60-second post-fill executable-midpoint movement where available, missingness and voids. Score reference consensus versus contemporaneous normalized Polymarket midpoints with paired match/block Brier and log-loss diagnostics. Forecast improvement is secondary, not a replacement for the P&L gate. Publish negative and inconclusive results as specified.

Paper success permits proposing a separately authorized tiny live fill-validation study; it does not authorize orders or establish a generalizable live edge. The protected holdout remains unused throughout this experiment.
