# Selected replacement experiment: executable soccer complete-set discounts

Selected 2026-10-13. Status: proposed prospective paper experiment; not launched. The bookmaker/API experiment remains cancelled. This design requires no new subscription, bookmaker account, API key, Tinker inference or real orders. Incremental paid-service budget is zero.

## Hypothesis

For an audited regulation-time home/draw/away contract triplet, an equal-quantity basket pays one dollar per complete set if exactly one of those outcomes wins under the archived resolution rules. Test whether a discount below that payout survives actual book depth, taker fees and non-atomic execution. This is a structural pricing hypothesis, not an improved soccer forecast. We have no evidence yet that executable opportunities occur often enough to matter. Other traders may already eliminate them.

## Frozen sample and signal

1. Collection plumbing gate: 20 future, chronologically first fixtures across EPL, La Liga, Serie A and Bundesliga with market mappings audited before kickoff. No trading or outcome inspection in this gate. Require valid, continuous three-book capture and complete rule/fee/size evidence for at least 16 fixtures. Otherwise stop as a data failure. Use the gate only to establish a conservative latency floor; no optimization of edge, universe or timing.
2. Freeze latency to `max(2 seconds, measured 99th-percentile public-request round-trip time from plumbing)`. A read-only round trip does not measure a signed order acknowledgment; retain this limitation. Add the market's documented/current matching delay for each leg and a fixed one-second acknowledgment/processing allowance before issuing the next leg.
3. Assessment: next 300 chronological scheduled fixtures in the same four leagues, or 60 days after assessment starts, whichever occurs first. Freeze the fixture manifest before price selection. Fixtures without mapped markets, missing captures, no signal or no fills remain in the all-fixture denominator. Do not replace them to improve results.
4. Observe only from scheduled kickoff minus 60 minutes through minus 5 minutes, using the kickoff available before the observation window. No in-play trading. A schedule/rules change invalidates trading for that fixture but does not remove it from the denominator.
5. Require exactly three exhaustive, mutually exclusive regulation outcomes, with YES token orientation and the same game/time/rules independently checked. The title or a generic negative-risk flag alone is insufficient. Exclude augmented/placeholder outcomes. Audit all specified cancellation, postponement and ambiguous-resolution payout states: if their combined basket payout cannot be bounded below by $1, the triplet is not tradable. Subsequent rule clarifications block further attempts and are reported.
6. At most one basket attempt per fixture, at the first qualifying observation. Validate each candidate with a public batch order-book read covering all three YES tokens, completed before the decision and received within one second; compare hashes with the continuously maintained stream and reject discrepancies or gaps. Use reconstructed states as of one common decision cutoff, with all updates received before that cutoff. An unchanged resting level need not have changed in the last second; the requirement is fresh validation of the book state, not a fictitious quote refresh. A batch read is not an atomic tradable basket, and subsequent fills still use separate arrival-time books. Unknown or changing constraints block the signal.
7. Request the smallest equal share quantity meeting all three markets' verified native quantity/notional/precision minima. Maximum total paper outlay including worst limit-price fees is $50 per basket, with a $200 aggregate outstanding paper-outlay cap. A cap-blocked fixture remains a no-trade observation. Capital is released only when actual development-market resolution/redemption availability is observed; unresolved exposure stays locked.
8. Set each original buy limit to its decision best ask plus one actual market tick, capped at 1. Require displayed depth to cover the requested quantity within these limits. Use the full-depth VWAP and each effective market-specific taker fee. Require both the estimated all-in cost AND the conservative all-limit-price/fee cost to be at most **$0.98 per complete set**. Thus at least two cents remain after fees and three one-tick execution allowances. Limits, quantity and fees must satisfy the outlay cap after rounding. Do not fit or search this two-cent threshold.

## Execution and accounting

- Three sequential fill-and-kill taker orders with original equal quantities and original limits; fixed order home, draw, away. Every leg gets one attempt, no resting remainder or retries. A batch API does not make these legs atomic. The next order is issued only after the previous modeled arrival/acknowledgment interval. A failed or partial leg is retained; do not remove its basket from results.
- Walk the recorded as-of book at each leg's modeled arrival, consuming depth within that leg's original limit. Continuous stream evidence must cover the relevant interval. Missing arrival evidence gives no credited fill. On fee/constraint/provenance loss after an earlier fill, stop subsequent legs and keep that residual exposure and its costs in the record. No price-touch fill assumption, simultaneous snapshot fills or hindsight order selection.
- Public-book fills are approximate paper executions: they do not prove our order would have beaten another trader or received a real acknowledgment. Report this explicitly even after a successful study.
- Hold all filled shares to the actual development-contract settlement; do not add an invented exit sale. Model actual redemption/settlement charges only when supported; a documented zero must be established, not guessed. No merge/conversion transaction, relayer subsidy, gas-free assumption or maker rebate is credited.
- Net P&L uses actual executable fill prices and separate taker fees; spread is already embedded in those prices. Report midpoint-to-execution attribution separately, without a second spread debit. Do not count each of the three contract legs as an independent winning bet.
- Let filled quantities be `q_home`, `q_draw`, `q_away`, and let `D` be total entry cash debited including fees. For ordinary valid regulation resolution, the conservative terminal P&L floor is **`min(q_home, q_draw, q_away) - D`**, less any supported settlement charges. This charges the entire acquisition cost, values unmatched excess shares at zero, and penalizes partial/failed baskets without selecting favorable match outcomes. Include all audited exceptional payout states when taking the floor; do not silently assume ordinary resolution. This is a conditional contract-payoff bound, not realized P&L or a bound on oracle/platform/collection failure.
- Mandatory stress replay keeps original signals, order limits, quantity and one-attempt rule. Add two seconds of delay to each leg and one extra tick of adverse execution price within the original limit. Cap each stressed leg's credited fill quantity at its primary replay quantity; no extra fills may be credited. Recompute fees and all partial-basket floors. Do not replace signals with stress-time discoveries.

## Success criterion and stop condition

Primary metric: mean conservative net terminal P&L floor per **all assessment fixtures**, versus holding cash. Aggregate every quote/order/leg into its match before inference. Preregister 10,000 bootstrap replicates with seed 20261013. Compute one-sided 95% lower confidence bounds using whole-match resampling and calendar-week block resampling; use the smaller bound. Blocks preserve some calendar/shared-team dependence, not all of it.

Paper success requires all of the following at the fixed endpoint:

- At least 20 distinct fixtures with all three requested legs completely filled, at least six assessment calendar weeks, and valid full-window capture on at least 80% of all assessment fixtures.
- The smaller lower bound on the mean conservative P&L floor is strictly positive in both primary and stress replay, with every partial/failed basket and all no-trade fixtures included.
- The accounting/receipt audit reconciles cash, inventory, fees, effective rules, arrival books and the exposure cap. Unresolved/changed-rule baskets cannot be declared realized profits; they block a success declaration until verified or are reported as inconclusive.

Stop early after the first 20 assessment fixtures only if fewer than 16 have valid captures or **zero** qualifying after-cost basket signals have appeared. The former is data failure; the latter is a negative opportunity-feasibility screen for this registered window, not proof that arbitrage never occurs. This screen cannot yield early success. If it passes, finish the fixed 300-fixture/60-day assessment without intermediate efficacy looks, positive early stopping, extensions or tuning. Too few completed baskets or a confidence bound crossing zero is inconclusive and ends this variant. Nonpositive mean is negative and ends it. Immediately stop on a protected-ID overlap, guard drift, unexpected paid-service requirement or loss of provenance.

Report signal prevalence, complete/partial/failed basket rates, conservative floors, realized development P&L separately when settled, fees, spread/slippage, worst floor/loss, turnover, aggregate outstanding exposure and capital lockup, both profitable-fixture denominators and post-fill executable-midpoint changes where observed. Repeated snapshots are not independent samples. No passing result automatically authorizes real orders; paper success would justify a separate small live fill-validation proposal.
